# 🚀 EchoBridge — Setup Guide

> **Quick Start for everyone:** No Python, no coding, no setup — just download and run.

---

## 📋 Requirements

- Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit)
- A working microphone
- Internet connection
- VRChat installed and running

---

## 📥 Step 1 — Download

1. Go to the [Releases page](https://github.com/Zpati/EchoBridge/releases)
2. Download `EchoBridge_v2.5.zip`
3. **Right-click** the zip → **Extract All** → Choose a folder (e.g. `Desktop\EchoBridge`)

---

## 🎮 Step 2 — Enable OSC in VRChat

> [!IMPORTANT]
> This step is required. Without OSC enabled, your translations will NOT appear in your VRChat chatbox.

1. Launch **VRChat**
2. Open the **Action Menu** (press **R** on keyboard or radial menu in VR)
3. Go to **Options** → **OSC**
4. Toggle **Enabled** → **ON**
5. Default OSC ports are automatically used (Input: **9000**).

---

## ▶️ Step 3 — Run the App

1. Open the extracted folder
2. Double-click **`EchoBridge.exe`**

> [!NOTE]
> Windows may show a SmartScreen warning ("Windows protected your PC").  
> Click **"More info"** → **"Run anyway"** — this is safe, the app is open source.

---

## 🌐 Step 4 — Configure the Translator

Once the app opens:

| Setting | What to do |
|---------|-----------|
| **Your Language** | Select the language YOU speak (e.g. Türkçe, Russian, Japanese) |
| **Target Language** | Select the language to translate INTO (e.g. English) |
| **Audio Device** | Select your preferred microphone from the dropdown |
| **Silence Timeout** | Adjust pause slider to match your speech cadence |

---

## 🎤 Step 5 — Start Translating

1. Click the toggle switch or press **`F4`**
2. Speak into your microphone in your language
3. Your speech will be:
   - Recognized automatically
   - Translated to the target language via dual-engine AI pipeline
   - Sent directly into your **VRChat avatar's chatbox**!

---

## ⌨️ Hotkeys

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| `F4` | Toggle Mic Translation ON/OFF |

The hotkey works globally — even when VRChat is the active focused window.

---

## 🌍 Change UI Language

Click the language button in the top-right corner of the app to switch between:
- 🇬🇧 **English**
- 🇹🇷 **Türkçe**
